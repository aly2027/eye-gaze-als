Powerful, simple

Communication that works the way you can move.

GazeSpeak pairs camera-based eye tracking with a smart, frequency-ordered board so the words you use most are always the fastest to reach.

Camera-based eye tracking

Uses the front camera of a standard phone or tablet. No headset, no infrared rig, no calibration lab required.

Frequency-ordered board

A Huffman-style layout keeps common words within the fewest glances, so everyday phrases come out faster.

Speaks out loud

Finished sentences are read aloud with built-in text-to-speech, in English or Korean, so caregivers hear them instantly.

Made for real use

Large targets, high-contrast text, and quick caregiver phrases designed with accessibility first — not as an afterthought.

How it works

Three glances from thought to voice.

Look to select

Rest your gaze on a zone of the board. GazeSpeak detects where you're looking and highlights it, then confirms with a short dwell.

Build the sentence

Each selection narrows toward the word you want. The most-used words sit closest, so full phrases take only a few moves.

Speak it out

Press play with a glance and the sentence is spoken aloud — letting you answer, ask, and connect in real time.
