See EYEUM in action

Three short videos recorded on a phone: how a person speaks with their eyes, how the practice mode works, and how caregivers can change phrases.

Captions:

The app screens in these videos are shown in English. EYEUM itself is available in English, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese and French.

1. Speaking with the eyes

Look left (0) or right (1) to build a short code, then hold the center to speak the phrase. Categories such as Pain open their own page.

2. Practice mode

A voice-guided practice in three steps, for people using EYEUM for the first time.

3. Setup: changing phrases

Caregivers can rewrite any phrase and choose how long the app waits before confirming a choice.
