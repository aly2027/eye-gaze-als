Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how the EYEUM app and website handle information. EYEUM is a communication tool that helps people with ALS build and speak sentences using eye movements. We designed it with privacy at its core.
The camera is used only on your device. EYEUM processes the camera image locally to detect where you are looking. Camera images and video are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. They stay on your device.

1. Information the App Collects

Camera and gaze data

The app uses your device's front camera to detect eye position for hands-free selection. This processing happens entirely on your device in real time. We do not record, save, upload, or share camera images, video, or gaze data.

App content

Any phrases you customize are stored locally on your device so the app can work for you. They are not sent to us.

The app does not require an account, and it does not collect your name, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.

2. Information the Website Collects

If you request the app through the download form on our website, you may choose to provide your email address and your role (for example, person with ALS, caregiver, or clinician). We also ask which country you are in, and you can answer “Prefer not to say”. We do not ask for your name or your organization.

We collect this only when you enter it yourself. It is kept in a private spreadsheet that only the project team can access.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide you with download links and setup support.
  • To send occasional updates about the app, if you asked for them.
  • To understand, in aggregate, who is using EYEUM so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Information you submit through the website is stored using Google services (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script). Google processes this data as our service provider. We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

EYEUM is intended for use by people with communication needs and their caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that requires consent we have not obtained. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will remove it.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you submitted through the website at any time. Because camera and gaze data never leave your device, there is nothing for us to delete on that side.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app develops. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us:

Email: contact.eyeum@gmail.com
